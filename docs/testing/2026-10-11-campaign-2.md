Marko's direction (2026-10-11): the credit is there to be used; run new websites, find more
errors. Candidates: `docs/testing/2026-10-11-campaign-2-candidates.md` (36, across Shopify,
BigCommerce, Magento, Salesforce Commerce and EU custom shops; Ikea as the control). The first
campaign's doc, `docs/testing/2026-10-08-credit-campaign.md`, is the model for the table and
the per-site notes.

## Where it lives

- **Organisation:** the same throwaway org as campaign 1, signed in as
  `credit-campaign@example.com` through `/login` (password `smoke`). Never Marko's address;
  never a new user.
- **Project:** a NEW project in that org, "Campaign 2 2026-10-11", with the same field list as
  campaign 1 (Title, Price, Main image, SKU, Brand, In stock, Rating, Description), so the two
  campaigns compare field by field. The campaign-1 project stays as it is.
- **Servers:** `pnpm dev:all` on the merged `main` (api-server :4000 with the Anthropic key, app
  :3000). The api-server opens its own Chromium for every capture and every coverage check.

## Rules for a worker

1. **One worker at a time, one Chromium of its own.** The machine has killed processes for
   memory three times this week with two or three browsers open. A worker drives the app with one
   headless Playwright page; the api-server's captures are the second browser; nothing else runs.
2. **Free steps freely; paid steps under the shown cap.** Add website, Find products, proof-page
   captures, marking, the coverage check, Sample and a 3-product Extract have cost $0 on every
   open site so far. Click Verify or Extract only when the button reads free / $0.00 / at most
   $0.50; otherwise screenshot the estimate, record it, and move on. A re-Verify after the
   coverage line's "Use as proof page" is the one paid step expected (the AI fallback runs when
   mechanical certification fails); record what it cost.
3. **Judge runs are the credit sink, and they run one at a time:**
   `pnpm --filter @robot/api exec tsx src/judge-run.ts <runId> --max-items 60 --max-usd 5`
   after an Extract of 60 products (budget "custom 60 products across custom 2 pages" or the
   nearest the tab offers). Never while another judge or the smoke runs. Record the report path.
4. **Never retry a wall, never re-run a paid step "for a better number".** A refused or
   challenged listing is a row in the table with its sentence and nothing more. A host backing
   off ("Waiting n min") is left alone; come back after the wait once, then record.
5. **Record what the product says, verbatim**, with timings and a screenshot per step into
   `docs/testing/results/campaign-2026-10-11/<site>-<step>.png`, and one note per site at
   `docs/testing/results/campaign-2026-10-11/<site>.md` (findings in the product first: anything
   wrong, odd, slow or misleading, with the exact words on screen).
6. **Nothing is "done" without evidence.** A Verify that passed is the row's "verified n/n"; an
   Extract is its row count and per-field fill; a judge run is its report's correctness table.
7. **No database writes outside the app.** Read-only SQL is fine for checking what was stored.
8. **If the api-server stops answering, stop and say so.** Never restart it.

## Order

1. **Pre-screen all 36**, free, through the app: add each website with its candidate listing,
   read the listing bar (the verdict sentence or "n products found · a pager"), the reachability
   line, screenshot, record. Walls are done at this point. About 36 × 1 minute.
2. **Verify the open ones**, in the candidate order: Find products, the first three products,
   accept what agrees, mark or type what does not, "Not on this product" where a field is really
   absent, Verify (free). Then wait for the coverage check (~4 min) and record its lines; use
   one "Use as proof page" where it names a gap, re-Verify (record the cost), record again.
3. **Sample and Extract** (3 products, then 60) on every verified site; record the run page's
   fill table and "Empty cells".
4. **Judge** every 60-product run, one at a time; record the correctness table and the judge's
   own limits (as campaign 1 did: star glyphs, "appears anywhere", vocabulary).
5. **Write it up** in this file's table and the "What campaign 2 answered" section, and a
   summary page (`/summarize`).
